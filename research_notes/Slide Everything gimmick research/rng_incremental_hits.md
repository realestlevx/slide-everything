# Roblox RNG-roll / collect-and-earn / base-income hits of 2025-2026: what the collectible was, and how theme drove virality

Research date: 2026-10-23. Every figure below comes from a public source. Most CCU numbers come from third-party trackers (Rolimons, RoStats, GGAID, RoWatcher, LiveCounts) or from the "RTC" news account, not from Roblox itself, and the trackers often disagree. Revenue figures are all third-party models or analyst estimates; Roblox does not report revenue per game. **RoStats caveat:** its "all-time peak" table only counts "since we started tracking each game", and it lists Steal a Brainrot at 1.41M, far below its 25M record. Treat RoStats peaks as the peak within its own tracking window, probably 2026 only.

## 1. Which games in this family hit big in 2025-2026 (numbers and dates)?

### Takeaway
Concurrent players (CCU) went through two waves. In 2025 a small set of meme- or cozy-themed "collect things on your plot and earn passively" games took the all-time record from 5M to 25M+ in five months: Grow a Garden, then Steal a Brainrot, then Plants vs Brainrots. In 2026 nothing came close to 25M. The biggest 2026 breakout is Steal An Egg, a "Steal a ___" follow-up from the same owner group as Steal a Brainrot, which peaked at about 14M on 2026-09-19. Around it sits a crowd of 1-4M "verb a noun" games. Pure RNG games (Sol's RNG) peaked roughly 100x lower than the stealing and growing games.

### Cited Findings

It lost the record to Steal a Brainrot in September 2025 — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- RTC account: a peak of 22,346,725, breaking GaG's own 21,334,520 — [RTC on X](https://x.com/Roblox_RTC/status/1959281995453706447)
- Fastest Roblox game to 1B visits (33 days). 35.3B+ visits and about 10.9M favourites as of May 2026. Jandel says about 35% of players are under 13 and declines to share revenue — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Revenue: about $150M in bookings in its first three months, a JPMorgan analyst estimate reported second-hand (unverified) — [DeepNewz](https://deepnewz.com/company-earnings/robloxs-grow-garden-hits-21-3m-peak-players-150m-bookings-created-teen-developer-2d7755c3)

### Inferences
- The 2025 mega-hits reached their peaks within 2-5 months of launch, and their peaks fell on update or event days. Two examples: GaG went from ~1K CCU (April) to 5M (May 18), and SAB went from launch (May 16) to 20M (August).
- Each mega-hit has a sharp peak and a fast decay. SAB went from 25M (October 2025) to about 130K (August 2026). GaG 2 lost about 66-90% of its players within 1-2 months. Peak CCU does not show durability. Revenue estimates suggest SAB's monthly earnings fell about 3-8x from its 2025 high.
- The 2026 successes were still "steal + collect + base income" (Steal An Egg) or simple "+1 stat" incrementals. None was a pure RNG roller.

### Gaps
- No primary (Roblox- or developer-reported) revenue for any of these games. All revenue numbers are models or analyst estimates.
- The date of 99 Nights' 14.2M peak was not found.
- Whether Grow a Garden 2 is the official sequel from Jandel/Splitting Point is unclear. Trackers list the developer as "Strawberreh Squad".
- Why so many games peaked on 2026-09-19 (Steal An Egg, +1 Speed Keyboard Escape, SAB) was not established. It may have been a platform-wide event. Unverified.

## 2. What was the collectible in each, and how much did theme matter versus mechanics? Is there evidence that a tight, meme-able collection beats a grab-bag?

### Takeaway
Every hit had a single, coherent, nameable collectible: brainrot characters, crops and fruit, pets from eggs, auras. In the biggest one (SAB), the collectibles were pre-existing viral meme characters, which children already knew and sang along to from TikTok. That borrowed recognition made the game clip-able and merch-able. No study directly compares a themed collection with a random-objects collection, but the circumstantial evidence all points one way. No random-objects collection appears among the 2025-2026 hits. The closest "everything" title, Eat Everything In A Grocery Store, spiked only to about 12K. Mechanics (stealing, PvP) were what kept players. Theme was what made the game spread.

### Cited Findings
**What each game collected**
- **SAB**: "Brainrots", voxel characters based on the Italian brainrot meme, sold on a conveyor and generating passive income — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- **Italian brainrot**: an AI-generated meme universe that started in 2025. Characters such as Tralalero Tralala, Bombardiro Crocodilo and Tung Tung Tung Sahur were famous before the game — [Forbes, 2025-09-19](https://www.forbes.com/sites/danidiplacido/2025/09/19/robloxs-italian-brainrot-trend-explained/)
- **GaG**: seeds, then crops (offline growth), plus pets from loot boxes — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- **PvB**: plants (seed rarities) that defend against brainrot waves; rare brainrots are collectibles; plants and brainrots fuse into rarer variants; card packs since October 2025. The article credits its success to combining three things:
  - GaG's farming, mutations and shop rotation
  - SAB's Italian brainrot memes and collectibles
  - Plants vs Zombies' lane defence

  It says the brainrot culture gives the game "shareable identity" — [Max Power Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/roblox-s-latest-breakout-game-is-plants-vs-brainrots)
- **Steal An Egg**: eggs that hatch into pets earning money every second, with rarer pets earning more. A chicken is the usual starter pet and dragons come after many hours. Rarer eggs in biomes are guarded by tougher guardians — [EGamers](https://egamers.io/robloxs-no-1-game-steal-an-egg-was-pulled-hours-after-reaching-the-top/)
- **Sol's RNG**: auras rolled with odds; the rarest are listed around 1/220,000,000 ("Oppression") — [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/roblox/news/what-is-the-rarest-aura-in-sols-rng)
- **Pet Simulator 99**: 2,000+ collectible pets — [Wikipedia: List of Roblox games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)

**How much the theme mattered**
- The theme became IP beyond the game. It produced:
  - a licensed Fortnite version — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/fortnite/creative-steal-the-brainrot-roblox-clone-1-million-players)
  - a film — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
  - 5.4M brainrot collectibles issued at the Bruno Mars concert — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
  - physical blind-box toys — [Jupiter & Dann review](https://jupiterhadley.com/steal-a-brainrot-blind-toys-review)

  GaG also got a film (announced November 2025) — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- The risk of a borrowed theme: SAB removed Tung Tung Tung Sahur in early September 2025 after a licensing dispute with its creator (Noxa / Mememtum Lab), and brought it back on 2025-11-29 — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/danidiplacido/2025/09/19/robloxs-italian-brainrot-trend-explained/)
- Mechanics explain stickiness. Naavik's podcast says the PvP stealing is what made SAB "so sticky", and suggests GaG faded partly because it is a PvE "cozy" game. That is a cross-platform discussion, not a dedicated analysis — [Naavik podcast](https://naavik.co/podcast/the-future-of-fortnite-creative/)
- Dubit (a Roblox studio) says the biggest new games usually ride a trend, giving internet culture such as "Brainrot" humour as an example. Its line is "evolution beats revolution": evolve a popular genre rather than invent one — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/what-makes-a-new-roblox-game-take-off)
- Counterpoint: one studio blog argues brainrot games convert well at launch but rarely keep players long term (opinion, unsourced) — [Game-Ace](https://game-ace.com/blog/roblox-game-ideas-that-actually-work/)
- The one "everything" title seen in tracker data, Eat Everything In A Grocery Store, rose from 1,441 to 12,145 CCU in the 24 hours after its 2026-10-17 update — [RoStats](https://rostats.io/records)
- Some 2026 hits have no collectible character set at all ("+1 Speed" escapes). Kick a Lucky Block's collectible is the randomness container (lucky blocks) — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/89469502395769)

### Inferences
- **Recognition did the marketing.** The SAB pattern is that the collectible already had name recognition and a sound (sing-song names) among under-13s. Rare pulls and thefts then became shareable clips without explanation. A grab-bag (Bread, Phone, D20, Lightsaber) has no shared identity, no characters, no names to chant and no "set" to complete, so each item is generic and a clip of it says nothing.
- **What's left without a meme.** Coherence within a set (all plants, all pets, all auras) seems to matter more than meme status. GaG's crops and Steal An Egg's pets are not memes, yet they read instantly as a set with an obvious rarity ladder (carrot to rare fruit; chicken to dragon).
- **Ways to make "everything" coherent** (these are design inferences, not evidence):
  1. Lean into absurdist "everything" as the meme: give items personalities, faces or sing-song names, or make the slide itself the character.
  2. Give each floor a themed sub-set: kitchen, toys, gamer gear, space.
  3. Use a rarity ladder that reads instantly as more valuable (Bread, then Golden Bread, then Cosmic Bread), possibly through mutations.
  4. Borrow a currently trending meme set, accepting the licensing risk SAB faced.
- **Where the slide fits.** The 2026 "+1 Speed" escape hits show that speed and momentum incrementals currently trend on their own. The slide's per-stud and speed loop may be the stronger hook, with items as the payload.

### Gaps
- No controlled or quantitative comparison of themed collections against grab-bag collections on Roblox exists in public sources.
- No retention or CCU data for any game whose collectibles are "random everyday objects", apart from the small Eat Everything In A Grocery Store spike.
- Did not confirm what Kick a Lucky Block's blocks contain (possibly brainrots; unverified).

## 3. What social and interaction hooks did they add on top of collect-and-earn?

### Takeaway
Five hooks recur:
1. Stealing from other players' bases, with defences.
2. Live "admin abuse" events tied to updates.
3. Server-wide weather and mutations.
4. Timed shop restocks and limited weekly items you must be online for.
5. Cross-game "wars" and brand events.

Trading exists, but the main headline drivers were stealing and live events.

### Cited Findings
**Stealing**
- SAB:
  - Stealing works like capture-the-flag.
  - Bases have a button that raises a temporary shield.
  - Traps and items can be bought with currency or Robux.
  - Rebirth resets progress for better stats.
  - Developers run "admin abuse" events with exclusive characters.

  [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Steal An Egg: base contents can be stolen by other players on the server. Speed is bought with Robux or earned on a treadmill — [EGamers](https://egamers.io/robloxs-no-1-game-steal-an-egg-was-pulled-hours-after-reaching-the-top/). Servers are capped at 7 players, "which makes thefts more personal" — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/). Player.One credits the PvP stealing ("thefts and chases create their own stories"), a quickly grasped objective, and about 15-minute sessions — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/robloxs-steal-egg-beating-some-platforms-biggest-games-164086)
- GaG: Robux can buy the ability to steal other players' crops — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden). GaG 2 added night-time stealing — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/)

**Live events, weather and timed stock**
- GaG admin abuse: Jandel joins a public server and triggers weather with mutation multipliers up to 150x, full seed and gear restocks, and free drops. It normally runs an hour before a new update, and the source says GaG first popularised the format on Roblox — [Followchain](https://www.followchain.org/admin-abuse-grow-a-garden/)
- GaG seed stock fluctuates, exclusive items release weekly, and players must be online to claim them — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- PvB runs weekly updates with weekend admin abuse: weather anomalies, rare brainrot spawns, restocks and limited items — [Max Power Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/roblox-s-latest-breakout-game-is-plants-vs-brainrots)

**Cross-game and brand events**
- On 2025-08-23 a GaG vs SAB "admin war" pushed Roblox to a record 47.4M CCU — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-peak-concurrent-user-count-hits-record-474m-as-steal-a-brainrot-and-grow-a-garden-compete/). A commercial source puts the head-to-head at GaG 21.1M vs SAB 20.1M — [U4GM](https://www.u4gm.com/steal-a-brainrot/blog-who-won-grow-a-garden-or-steal-a-brainrot-in-the-admin-abuse-war) (low-quality source)
- The SAB Bruno Mars concert (January 2026) drew 12.8M CCU — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)

**Platform signals**
- Roblox's 2026 discovery signals include "intentional co-play days", meaning players joining with friends — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/what-makes-a-new-roblox-game-take-off)
- Roblox's 2025 roadmap said the Recommended For You algorithm optimises for long-term retention — [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox%E2%80%99s-2025-discovery-roadmap-and-ways-to-reach-and-engage-your-audience/3557533)

### Inferences
- Slide Everything is currently single-player in its interactions: your slots, your slide. Mapping the hits' hooks onto it gives:
  - a public, server-visible slide or leaderboard ("who slid furthest this hour")
  - server-wide weather or mutation events that change slide multipliers or item mutations
  - timed roll-stand restocks with limited items
  - developer-hosted "admin abuse" sessions on update days
  - a light PvP or interference mechanic, such as knocking or stealing items off other players' slides or slots
- PvP stealing is the hook most associated with stickiness and with the record numbers.

### Gaps
- No data on how much trading specifically contributed to any of these games' numbers.
- No primary developer post-mortem (DevForum or RDC talk) explaining these hook choices was found in this pass.

## 4. How did they stage rarity reveals and "rare pull" moments for streamers and TikTok?

### Takeaway
The hits stacked rarity above "Mythic" into themed, named top tiers, and made rare events public. Sol's RNG plays a cutscene and announces rare pulls server-wide. GaG and PvB create shared moments from server-wide weather and admin drops. SAB's viral clips were often emotional, such as children crying after losing a brainrot to theft. Creator and brand events then multiplied the reach.

### Cited Findings
**Rarity tiers above Mythic**
- SAB's tiers are usually listed as Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Brainrot God, Secret and OG. That is three themed tiers above Mythic, and sources disagree on the exact count — [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/all-brainrots-in-steal-a-brainrot/); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/all-secrets-steal-brainrot)
- Brainrot God and Secret are the tiers with extremely low spawn chance — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/all-secrets-steal-brainrot)

**Sol's RNG reveals**
- Rare rolls often trigger a special cutscene and may be announced to the whole server. One cited global-chat notification was at 1/250,000, and the top auras are listed around 1/220,000,000 — [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/roblox/news/what-is-the-rarest-aura-in-sols-rng). The sources are guide sites; exact thresholds are not confirmed.

**Clips and creator reach**
- SAB built up tens of millions of views on TikTok and YouTube, including clips of children crying after losing their brainrots, and was popularised through shared strategy tips and viral clips — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Wikipedia: List of Roblox games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- PC Gamer called SAB's admin abuse events a "sensory overload" — via [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- A single YouTube video (Foltyn, 1.3M views) spread screenshots of Steal An Egg's treadmill "Reels" feed, which helped make it notorious — [EGamers](https://egamers.io/robloxs-no-1-game-steal-an-egg-was-pulled-hours-after-reaching-the-top/)

**Weather and mutation moments**
- GaG admin weather turns crops "Meteoric" when meteors hit them, and admin weather mutation multipliers reach 150x — [Followchain](https://www.followchain.org/admin-abuse-grow-a-garden/)

### Inferences
- Slide Everything's 1-in-3,000,000 Mythics are clip material only if the reveal is staged. Elements to consider:
  - a cutscene
  - a server-wide announcement above a set odds threshold
  - a distinct sound
  - a visible on-base display
  - a first-of-server or first-ever badge
- The slide adds a second reveal: watching a rare item smash through multiplier walls is a natural vertical-video moment, as long as the payout visibly ramps.
- Generic tier names like "Mythic" are weaker than themed top tiers such as SAB's "Brainrot God", "Secret" or "OG". Themed names give players words to shout in titles and thumbnails.

### Gaps
- No primary source (developer interview or RDC talk) on how these teams deliberately designed reveals for clips.
- Sol's RNG cutscene and global-announcement details come from low-quality guide sites, and the odds conflict between them.

## 6. How have the clones and copycats performed? Is "roll + base income" saturated by late 2026, and what set apart the clones that still succeeded?

### Takeaway
The 2025 viral format is clearly past its peak:
- Roblox itself blamed a 2026 bookings slowdown on players moving away from "highly monetized viral games".
- It changed discovery to reward 28-day retention.
- The 2025 originals are down 90%+ from their peaks.

But follow-ups did succeed. The biggest ones came from the original teams or owners (PvB from GaG's owner; Steal An Egg from SAB's owner, DoBig), and each changed the collectible and added a twist rather than reskinning. A clone of a clone (Break and Steal an Egg, 1.3M) still worked in October 2026. Clones with no differentiation mostly ended up in lawsuits, not on the charts.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- "Roll + base income" on its own is saturated and, since June 2026, algorithmically disadvantaged when it only produces a spike. What still breaks out in late 2026 is the format with:
  1. a new, coherent collectible
  2. a PvP or social twist (stealing, small servers)
  3. a new physical verb or stat (break, kick, ride, +1 speed)
  4. operators who already know the live-event playbook
- For Slide Everything, the slide (a physical, visual, momentum-based payout) is the differentiating verb, closer to the "+1 Speed", "Kick a Lucky Block" and "Ride A Pet" style of 2026 hits than to plain roll tycoons. The theme question is less "everything vs brainrot" than whether the items form a recognisable, chantable, collectable set with themed top tiers.
- Copying the collectible (brainrot characters) invites legal and licensing risk. SAB itself lost a character to a licensing dispute, and its owners actively sue copycats.
