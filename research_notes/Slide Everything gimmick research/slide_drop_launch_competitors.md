# Slide / Drop / Launch / Throw / Kick-for-Money Games on Roblox: Competitive Landscape (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-23. "Observed 2026-10-23" means the Rolimons page was fetched on that day. Rolimons shows relative dates ("8 months ago"), so creation dates are given only where a source stated them. Stats differ between trackers (Rolimons, RoTrends, Profitable, RoWatcher, fan wikis) because they were captured at different times. Where they conflict, both figures are given.

## 1. Which notable Roblox games exist in this space, and what are their stats?

### Takeaway
The niche splits into three tiers. Mega-hits (1M+ peak CCU) use distance only as a way to get a rarer collectible: Escape Tsunami For Brainrots peaked at about 5.0M CCU in January 2026, and Kick a Lucky Block peaked at about 1.69M CCU in May 2026. Mid-size physics/ragdoll games (roughly 13K-57K peak) build on physics comedy. Most direct "distance = cash" games (slide, throw, ramp jump, marble slide, Plinko) peak at only about 1.7K-25K CCU and fade to tens or hundreds of players.

### Inferences
- Peak CCU follows the hook, not the physical verb. Every "distance = cash" game found (slide, throw, ramp, marble slide, Plinko) peaked between about 1.7K and 25K. The two distance-mechanic games that reached 1M+ CCU (Escape Tsunami, Kick a Lucky Block) attached distance to brainrot collection, a plot with passive income, and a risk timer (tsunami).

- Favorites-to-visits ratios are high for the small direct-distance games (Be a Car about 5.7%, Build a Marble Slide about 2.7%, Throwing Simulator about 2.4%). These are my own calculations from the Rolimons figures above. They suggest players like these concepts but are not retained by them.

## 2. Which games broke out in 2025-2026, and what was the specific hook?

### Takeaway
The breakouts were meme-IP (brainrot) collection games that added an active, physical "go far" moment with risk: running from a tsunami, or kicking a lucky block and racing back before the wave. The second breakout tier was ragdoll bone-breaking comedy (Break your Bones, 56.8K peak in Sept 2025). No pure slide, throw or ramp game broke out; the best 2026 entrant, Throwing Simulator, peaked at 9.6K.

### Cited Findings
- Escape Tsunami For Brainrots' loop is "Collect Brainrots → Survive Tsunamis → Upgrade → Repeat". The map is split into zones, and brainrots are worth more the farther the player gets. Deeper zones have tighter escape windows. A wave that hits you ends the run. Speed upgrades are reset by rebirths that give permanent money multipliers — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-roblox-s-next-brainrot-juggernaut)
- MaxPowerGaming's reasons for the breakout: a mash-up of Steal a Brainrot and the older *Be Crushed by a Speeding Wall*; frequent updates, limited-time modes and admin-abuse events; ultra-rare brainrots as long-term goals; optional stealing and pay-to-win; leaderboards; "close-call moments that suit streamers and YouTubers"; and a simultaneous launch on Fortnite Creative — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-roblox-s-next-brainrot-juggernaut)
- Kick a Lucky Block (launched April 7, 2026; 1.69M peak on May 23, 2026). Kick power sets how far the lucky block travels, and landing distance sets the zone and brainrot tier (11 zones from Common to Celestial). Weights raise kick power; run speed keeps the longer returns survivable; the first rebirth unlocks at 1,000 Kick Power and gives a permanent 2x cash multiplier; a 99 Robux "Rebirth Skip" exists — [kickaluckyblock.wiki (fan wiki)](https://www.kickaluckyblock.wiki/)
- Earnaldo describes Kick a Lucky Block differently: at high power, blocks can shatter mid-flight and rain brainrots; a tsunami that catches you loses that round's brainrots; it does not say that distance changes rarity — [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/break-a-lucky-block-vs-kick-a-lucky-block). This partly conflicts with the fan wiki's distance-to-zone claim.
- Break your Bones (created June 5, 2025) peaked at 56,813 CCU on Sept 13, 2025: a "bone smashing simulator" with unlockable ragdolls — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/123821081589134), [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/123821081589134/Break-your-Bones)
- 2026 breakouts in the direct-distance sub-niche were small: Throwing Simulator (created Feb 18, 2026) peaked at 9,639 on May 2, 2026, and Build a Marble Slide peaked at 4,560 on Feb 16, 2026 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/90977176306153), [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/84907238849255)
- Plinko-style RNG: the strongest 2026 entry (Ball Game) peaked at 5,318 on July 11, 2026 and showed 0 players on 2026-10-23 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/72016039587124)

### Inferences
- The hooks behind the breakouts are: (a) a recognisable meme cast (brainrots) as the collectible; (b) distance or depth as a rarity dial, so going farther feels like rolling better; (c) a risk/escape timer that creates close calls for streamers; (d) a plot with passive income; and (e) rebirth multipliers. Kick a Lucky Block is structurally very close to Slide Everything (roll → place for income → train to go farther). The main differences are that its distance picks the rarity tier, it has a fail state, and it uses brainrot IP.
- "Absurd objects" did not appear as a breakout hook in its own right in 2025-2026. Comedy came from ragdoll physics (Break your Bones, Fling Things and People) or from brainrot memes, not from everyday-object variety.

### Gaps
- No primary analysis (Roblox Creator Hub, Bloxbiz, Naavik) of Kick a Lucky Block's or Escape Tsunami's retention or monetization was found. The explanations above come from fan, aggregator and blog sources.
- Whether Kick a Lucky Block's distance directly sets rarity is supported by the fan wiki only; Earnaldo does not confirm it.

## 3. Does "what you launch matters" (different objects with different value or behaviour) appear in successful games, or is it usually the player sliding?

### Takeaway
Both exist, but neither is the hook in the hits. In the mega-hits, the player's own body is the vehicle (running, kicking), and object variety lives in the collectible (brainrots by rarity), not in how things travel. In the small direct-distance games, object or vehicle variety is a standard upgrade path (heavier items, bigger rides, better cars, rarer track pieces), not a headline hook.

### Cited Findings
- Throwing Simulator: "buy heavier items" is part of the loop, alongside strength training and pets — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/90977176306153)
- Slide Down A Hill: the player rides objects (carts, props, odd vehicles) down the slide, and money buys "bigger and better rides" — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/13682880624), [RobloxDen](https://robloxden.com/game-codes/slide-down-a-hill)
- Be a Car: the player is or drives the car; upgrades are a taller ramp and a faster car — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/107326628277908)
- Build a Marble Slide: the player rides a slide they built themselves; variety is in rare track pieces and trading — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/84907238849255)
- Break your Bones: variety is in unlockable ragdolls (the player's body) — [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/123821081589134/Break-your-Bones)
- Kick a Lucky Block: the kicked object (lucky block) is generic; its contents (brainrots) carry the value — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/89469502395769)
- MEGA RAMP FOR SLIME: the car or player does the ramp, and the reward is a collectible slime that earns per second when placed — [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/92700582787930/MEGA-RAMP-FOR-SLIME)
- YouTube: a video titled "We Slide EVERYTHING Down A Hill In Roblox!" exists, which shows that "slide everything" is already used as a creator video hook. Channel, date, views and the game featured could not be retrieved — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d2PYiQMw_kI)

### Inferences
- "Slide Everything" combines two patterns: the item is both the collectible (rolled with RNG, placed for income) and the projectile (slid for distance). Among the games found, only Throwing Simulator ("heavier items") and Slide Down A Hill ("rides") let the object change travel, and neither reached hit scale. In the hits, the thing that travels and the thing that is collected are separate (block or body vs brainrot).
- The "everything" angle is weaker as a recognisability hook than brainrots: Pocket Gamer's gate-runner advice says to "pick a theme that people already recognize from everyday life, often drawn from social media". Everyday objects (bread, phone, Rubik's cube) are recognisable but not memes. Brainrot characters, or other meme-native items, are both. (This is an inference that applies mobile advice to Roblox.)
- The YouTube title suggests "slide EVERYTHING" reads well as a thumbnail or video premise, which is a point in favour of the angle for creator coverage. It is unverified how many views that video got.

### Gaps
- I could not find which game the "We Slide EVERYTHING Down A Hill" video covers, or its view count.
- No source compared retention between "object matters" and "player slides" designs.

## 4. How do these games handle the moment-to-moment run (passive, steered, timed, skill), and how do multiplier gates/walls show up on Roblox?

### Takeaway
The hits use active, risky runs: running from a tsunami, and in Kick a Lucky Block a timing-bar "Perfect Kick" plus a race back. Small distance games are mostly physics you watch (ragdoll or object survival), or a stat-driven throw or jump. On Roblox, "multiplier gate" mechanics appear mainly as Plinko peg multipliers and multiplier bands, and as rebirth or gamepass multipliers. No Roblox gate-runner hit (Count Masters style) was found.

### Cited Findings
- Kick a Lucky Block: "Land the meter in the green zone instead of releasing early" gives a Perfect Kick with maximum launch power and extra reach. The return trip under tsunami threat is a speed and risk test — [kickaluckyblock.wiki](https://www.kickaluckyblock.wiki/). Timing matters mainly for the tsunami: keep kicking for a nearby rare drop, or flee to high ground — [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/break-a-lucky-block-vs-kick-a-lucky-block)
- Escape Tsunami: an active run through zones to "small safe pockets or gaps before the wave arrives", which get harder to reach farther out — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-roblox-s-next-brainrot-juggernaut)
- Slide Down A Hill: physics survival. "Lasting longer on each slide down earns you more money" (ride objects, try to survive) — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/13682880624), [RobloxDen](https://robloxden.com/game-codes/slide-down-a-hill)
- Broken Bones IV: payout depends on impact speed and surface hardness; a slow tumble down snow earns almost nothing, while a high-speed slam into rock breaks several bones — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/broken-bones-iv-on-roblox-how-to-play-and-earn-cash/) (via search summary)
- Fall and Break Bones!: 2x cash if you reach the ground without getting stuck between obstacles, a light skill bonus — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/fall-break-bones-a-beginner-s-guide), [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/122819831761125/Fall-and-Break-Bones)
- Ball Game (Plinko): its 7/23/26 update added "Critical Drop, Peg Multiplier, and Multiplier Band", the closest Roblox match found to multiplier walls on a drop path — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/72016039587124)
- Search found no Roblox runner using "math gates" or "multiplier walls". Multipliers on Roblox mostly show up as stackable x2/x5 gamepasses, trail multipliers (Steal an Egg's x5 Galaxy Trail, x14 Divine Trail) and rebirth multipliers — [Super Power Training Simulator wiki](https://roblox-super-power-training-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Multipliers), [Games.gg Steal an Egg guide](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/steal-an-egg-speed-how-to-increase-speed-fast/), [Rolimons x5 pass](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/6512739)
- Mobile gate runners, for reference: per Data.ai, 3 of the top 10 US mobile games in 2022 were gate runners (Crowd Evolution, Tall Man Run, Count Masters), with about 500M cumulative downloads. Choosing gates adds decision-making; visual changes are exaggerated; levels last about 30 seconds; a 5-second clip shows many changes, which helps CPI and ad creatives. Advice: use recognisable everyday or social-media themes, make changes exaggerated, and combine with other genres — [PocketGamer.biz, Uri Ron (ironSource), Nov 16, 2022](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/the-method-in-the-madness-how-to-make-a-successful-gate-runner-game/)

### Inferences
- Slide Everything's steer-into-multiplier-walls design is a skill layer that most Roblox slide and throw games lack, since they rely on stats or watched physics. It sits in a gap between passive Plinko (Peg Multiplier and Multiplier Band, watched RNG) and the active risk loops of the hits.
- What the hits have and Slide Everything lacks is stakes: a fail state or risk (tsunami) that creates close calls, which MaxPowerGaming names as a streamer driver. Multiplier walls give upside but no downside. Pocket Gamer's gate-runner analysis says the decision (choose a gate) is the engaging part, so steering between competing walls (e.g. x2 vs +distance, or a risky lane vs a safe lane) fits that model better than walls that can only help. (Inference.)
- Gate runners worked partly because of exaggerated visual change per gate. Applied to Roblox, a multiplier wall should visibly change the item (grow it, set it on fire, clone it) and not only change a number. (Inference from the mobile source, not tested on Roblox.)

### Gaps
- No Roblox-specific data was found on whether steering or skill improves retention versus passive watching in this niche.
- No Roblox game was found that copies the "x2 / x5 gate" runner mechanic as its core; absence from search does not prove there is none.

## 5. What is oversaturated and what is underexplored in this niche right now?

### Takeaway
Oversaturated: brainrot reskins using "[Verb] a Brainrot" or "[Verb] a Lucky Block" naming, and Plinko/ball-drop RNG games. Each has several clones with low hundreds to low thousands of peak CCU, and a February 2026 DevForum market analysis calls the brainrot format peaked. Underexplored, with low competition and also low proven demand: a distance or slide game where the item is both collectible and projectile, with player skill (steering into multipliers). No 2025-2026 slide game with significant CCU was found.

### Cited Findings
- DevForum "Market Friday" genre tracker (posted Feb 20, 2026, updated Feb 23, 2026): Brainrot 1.4M total CCU across 29 tracked games; Escape Games 763.6K across 15; Simulator 336.3K across 17. ### Inferences
- **Crowded:** brainrot plus a verb ("Throw/Drop/Kick/Slide a Brainrot"); Plinko RNG; lucky-block openers; tsunami-escape clones. A "Slide a Brainrot" pitch would land in the most crowded lane, though it is also where demand provably is.
- **Open but unproven:** the slide as a skill course (steering, choosing multiplier lanes) with collectible items that each slide differently. Competitors either watch physics (Slide Down A Hill, ragdoll games) or use stat-gated throws (Throwing Simulator, Be a Car). The empty space may mean opportunity, or it may mean low demand: every direct-distance game found peaked under about 25K.
- **Positioning advice from the evidence:** Slide Everything's systems (roll stands, passive slots, luck, rebirth-style upgrades) already match the brainrot formula that Kick a Lucky Block rode to 1.69M. The likely missing pieces are (1) a meme-recognisable item cast, or a theme legible in one thumbnail; (2) a risk or stakes beat during the slide; and (3) distance feeding rarity or rolls, not only cash. "Everything" as a hook mainly helps thumbnails and YouTube titles (see "We Slide EVERYTHING Down A Hill"); none of the found hits used it as their retention hook.
